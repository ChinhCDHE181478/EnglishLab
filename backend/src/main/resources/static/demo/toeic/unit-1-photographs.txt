ENGLISHLAB TOEIC 650 - UNIT 1: PHOTOGRAPHS

Learning goals
- Identify people, objects, locations, and actions in workplace photographs.
- Distinguish present continuous from passive descriptions.
- Avoid distractors that mention visible nouns but describe the wrong action.

Practice routine
1. Scan the whole image before listening.
2. Predict one verb and one location phrase.
3. Eliminate statements containing unsupported details.

Homework: complete the Unit 1 system quiz in EnglishLab.
